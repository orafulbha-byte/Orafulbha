Give With Confidence

Every gift, large or small, helps ORAFULBHA support vulnerable people, promote education, provide relief and strengthen communities.

We do not collect payments through this website. Instead, we ask you to get in touch first, so you can ask any questions and receive our giving details directly from us.

How to donate

  1. Tell us you would like to give. Use the form on this page, or email orafulbha@gmail.com.
  2. We reply to you directly with the ways you can give.
  3. Ask us anything. You are welcome to ask how your gift will be used before you decide.

I would like to donate

This does not make a payment. It prepares an email to us.
