About Us

Who we are and what we believe.

Who we are

ORAFULBHA — Global Humanitarian Network

ORAFULBHA is a humanitarian organization dedicated to helping vulnerable people, supporting communities, promoting education, providing relief and improving people's lives.

Our tagline, Empowering People. Transforming Lives., describes what we hope to do: not only to meet urgent needs, but to help people gain the strength, knowledge and support to build a better future for themselves.

What we believe

Our Guiding Beliefs

🕊️

Every life has dignity

Every person deserves to be treated with respect, no matter who they are or where they come from.

🌱

Help should empower

The best support helps people grow stronger and more independent over time.

🤲

Communities lead

Lasting change happens when we listen to communities and work alongside them.

What we do

Our Areas of Work

  • Support for vulnerable people and families
  • Promoting education and learning
  • Providing relief in times of hardship
  • Supporting and strengthening communities
